# Future roadmap

## Immediately after the hackathon

- Geospatial nearby search and explicit helper availability preferences
- Verified contact methods (accounts are not currently identity-verified)
- Leaflet map view and category/urgency filters
- Image attachments and AI-assisted classification

## Product expansion

- Private in-app chat after a request is accepted
- Volunteer badges, reputation, and moderation dashboard
- Multilingual and voice-led request creation
- Disaster mode with shelters and verified organisation broadcasts
- Partnerships with NGOs, hospitals, local services, and civic authorities

## Guardrails before scale

- Privacy review for exact location sharing
- Clear emergency-service escalation messaging
- Abuse prevention and human moderation workflow
- Accessibility and low-bandwidth support
