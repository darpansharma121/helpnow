# Problem statement

When someone needs urgent local help—a blood donor, a mechanic after a breakdown, medicine, a lost item, or assistance during flooding—they commonly post unstructured messages in WhatsApp or social-media groups.

Those messages are hard to discover, often lack exact context or location, and disappear quickly in busy conversations. Potential helpers cannot filter by urgency, distance, or capability; requesters cannot tell whether anyone has taken ownership.

HelpNow provides a focused alternative: structured requests, approximate location, real-time visibility, and an explicit acceptance/status flow. It is not a replacement for emergency services; users must still call official emergency numbers whenever there is immediate danger.
