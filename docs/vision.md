# Vision

## Mission

Make it fast and trustworthy for communities to connect people who need urgent local help with people who can provide it.

## Product vision

In a stressful moment, people should not have to guess which group to post in or scroll through unrelated messages. HelpNow turns a request into a location-aware, structured signal that reaches nearby, relevant helpers.

## Target users

- Residents needing time-sensitive assistance
- Volunteers and neighbours willing to help
- Local service providers such as mechanics and pharmacies
- Community organisations and disaster-response coordinators

## Principles

- **Speed:** a request should take under one minute to publish.
- **Local relevance:** show nearby, applicable requests first.
- **Safety:** share only the minimum location and contact information necessary.
- **Clarity:** urgent requests must be easy to understand and act on.
- **Trust:** status, ownership, and moderation make requests accountable.

## Hackathon success criteria

During a live demo, a user can create a request, another user can immediately see and accept it, and both clients reflect its new status without a page refresh.
